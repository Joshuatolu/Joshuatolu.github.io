Adventist Hymnal privacy policy

Last updated 13 October 2026

Adventist Hymnal collects no personal data. It has no accounts, analytics, advertising or tracking.

What stays on your phone

Your settings, favourites, name for the greeting, and any corrections or songs you choose to Save on this phone are stored only on your phone. If Android backup is turned on, Android may include them in your own Google backup; the developer cannot see it.

What is sent, and only when you choose to

The app uses the internet for two things:

  1. Sending a correction or a song for review, only when you tap Save and send for review. What is sent: the words you wrote, the tune file if you added one, which hymn it corrects, and a random number made by the app on your phone (not your name, phone number or any account). The server keeps that random number and your network address only in a scrambled (hashed) form, to limit how much one phone can send per day. On phones with Google Play, Google Play Integrity is used to confirm the request comes from the genuine app; Google processes device information for that check under Google's own privacy policy.
  2. Downloading approved corrections and songs, which everyone receives. This sends nothing about you.

Submissions are read by the Adventist Hymnal reviewers. Approved ones are published in the app for everyone, without any information about who sent them. Rejected ones are never published: their tune is deleted, and their words are kept only as a record of the decision. The server is hosted by Supabase.

Donations and ratings

Donate pays through Google Play. Google handles the payment under its own privacy policy; the app never sees your card or bank details. Like this app? opens the app's Play Store page; any rating or review you leave there is between you and Google Play.

Music

Playing a hymn uses the phone's audio only; the app pauses it when a call comes in.

Contact and deletion

To ask for a submission to be deleted, or with any question about privacy, write to toluseonibiyo@gmail.com.
