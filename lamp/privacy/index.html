Lamp privacy policy

Last updated 15 November 2026

Lamp is a Bible app. It collects no personal data. It has no accounts, analytics, advertising or tracking. Reading works without the internet.

What stays on your phone

Your name (if you give one, for the greeting), your settings, bookmarks, highlights, notes and reading place are stored only on your phone. They are never sent to the developer or anyone else.

For Sabbath hours and the automatic day and night appearance, you can choose your city from a list in the app (it is first suggested from your phone's time zone). Sunrise and sunset are worked out on your phone. Lamp does not use your location.

Backup

If Android backup is turned on, Android may include Lamp's settings, bookmarks, highlights and notes in your own Google backup, so they come back on a new phone. That backup belongs to your Google account and is handled by Google under Google's privacy policy; the developer cannot see it.

Reminders and the widget

The daily reminder, the Friday Sabbath reminder (each only if you turn it on) and the verse-of-the-day widget are prepared on your phone. Nothing about them leaves your phone.

Text from other apps

If you choose Lamp for text selected in another app, or share text to Lamp, the text is read on your phone to find the Bible reference in it. It is not kept or sent anywhere.

Listening and the internet

Lamp uses the internet only to fetch Bible audio when you choose to listen. It downloads the recording, keeps it on your phone, and sends nothing about you. Like any download, the request reaches the server that holds the recordings (Cloudflare), which may briefly keep ordinary technical logs; the developer does not use them.

When you listen to a version without a recording, your phone's own text-to-speech voice reads the text on your phone.

Gifts and ratings

A gift in the app is paid through Google Play. Google handles the payment under its own privacy policy; the app never sees your card or bank details, and the developer receives only Google Play's usual sales reports. Rate Lamp opens the app's Play Store page; any rating or review you leave there is between you and Google Play. Send feedback opens your own email app; what you write is sent only if you send it.

Children

Lamp is suitable for all ages and collects no information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be published at this address with a new date.
