Lamp privacy policy

Last updated 6 November 2026

Lamp is a Bible app. It collects no personal data. It has no accounts, analytics, advertising or tracking, and it works without the internet.

What stays on your phone

Your name (if you give one, for the greeting), your settings, bookmarks, highlights, notes and reading place are stored only on your phone. They are never sent to the developer or anyone else.

Backup

If Android backup is turned on, Android may include Lamp's settings, bookmarks, highlights and notes in your own Google backup, so they come back on a new phone. That backup belongs to your Google account and is handled by Google under Google's privacy policy; the developer cannot see it.

Reminders and the widget

The daily reminder (only if you turn it on) and the verse-of-the-day widget are prepared on your phone. Nothing about them leaves your phone.

Gifts and ratings

A gift in the app is paid through Google Play. Google handles the payment under its own privacy policy; the app never sees your card or bank details. Rate Lamp opens the app's Play Store page; any rating or review you leave there is between you and Google Play. Send feedback opens your own email app; what you write is sent only if you send it.

Children

Lamp is suitable for all ages and collects no information from anyone, including children.

Contact

With any question about privacy, write to toluseonibiyo@gmail.com.
