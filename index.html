Adventist Hymnal

Android · coming to Google Play

The difficulty: church members keep a separate hymn app for each language they sing in.

The solution: the Seventh-day Adventist hymnals of many languages in one app. The English and Yorùbá hymnals sit side by side, so any hymn switches language with one tap while the piano keeps playing.

695 English hymns · 621 Yorùbá hymns · 225 responsive readings

  • Piano accompaniment for every English hymn
  • Find a hymn by number, title or a line of its words
  • Works offline, with no account, adverts or tracking
  • Readers suggest corrections; reviewed changes reach every phone as signed updates

Flutter · Dart · Supabase · Ed25519-signed updates

SapaProof

Android · preparing for Google Play

The difficulty: salary lands and is gone before month end, and bank charges slip by unnoticed.

The solution: a personal finance app for Nigerians that reads bank alerts on the phone, shows where the money goes, including the charges the bank never mentioned, and gives advice the way a Nigerian parent would.

  • Reads alerts from First Bank, GTBank and Union Bank, with a general reader for other banks and wallets
  • Imports bank statements in PDF or Word
  • Budgets, savings goals, loans and recurring payments
  • No internet permission: data stays on the phone, encrypted

Kotlin · Jetpack Compose · SQLCipher

Awa Church

Web app · church management and finance

The difficulty: church records, tithes and offerings kept in notebooks and spreadsheets, with the treasurer's sums checked by hand.

The solution: one system for a church's members and money, first built for Lawanson Adventist Church.

  • Double-entry accounting with a chart of accounts and a trial balance
  • Sabbath tithes and offerings shared automatically between church, conference and district
  • Member profiles with families, ministries and QR-code attendance
  • Bulk SMS, financial and membership reports
  • Roles for treasurer, secretary and admins, with an audit trail

Laravel · PHP · MySQL · Tailwind CSS · Chart.js
